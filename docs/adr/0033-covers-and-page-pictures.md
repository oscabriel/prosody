# A cover never stops a copy, and a PDF's pages are pictures

The cover is one JPEG no taller than 800 pixels, from the picture the EPUB names, or else the one the audio carries, or else a PDF's first page; a book with no picture, or one that will not convert, simply has none. A PDF's pages are drawn as JPEGs 1,600 pixels on their longer side, sharp on a phone and a laptop, with names padded so they sort in order, and only the top level of its outline becomes headings, as a book's contents page leaves the rest out.
