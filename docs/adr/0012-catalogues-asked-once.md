# The catalogues outside are asked once, by title and author alone

When you want a book, Open Library, Audible's catalogue and Audnexus are asked once and the work is never looked up again. None needs an account and none is sent anything but a title and an author; a file's embedded title is never trusted. A name found may complete the one you gave and never replace it. Open Library has 8 seconds because the platform reruns a policy that fails within 10; the audiobook's lookups have 30 and yield no audiobook when they fail. Audible is asked only because Audnexus has no search.
