# Acquiring

The words for fetching a book you want: searched for, offered, grabbed, downloaded and shelved. The rest of the project's words are in the [root glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## Language

### Wanting and grabbing

**Search**:
One asking of the Indexer for a want. Each has its own number, and only the latest one's answer counts.
_Avoid_: Query, lookup

**Release**:
One upload of a book on a tracker, as the Indexer describes it.
_Avoid_: Torrent, result, hit

**Offer**:
A release the latest search found for a want. Only an offer can be grabbed.
_Avoid_: Result, candidate

**Grab**:
One release you chose, from its download to the end of its seeding. Only you grab; nothing does it for you.
_Avoid_: Download (for the whole), request, order

**Download**:
The transfer of a grab's payload. It is one part of a grab's life.
_Avoid_: Grab, fetch

**Stall**:
A download that has stopped coming, with the reason. It waits for you to resume or abandon it.
_Avoid_: Failure, error, timeout

**Payload**:
The file or folder a grab's download brought, in the downloads. It is never moved or deleted.
_Avoid_: Torrent, download, files

**The downloads**:
The folder on the host where payloads land.
_Avoid_: Torrents folder, incoming

**Obligation**:
What your tracker asks of a payload before it may stop seeding.
_Avoid_: Ratio, seed time, rule

**Release a payload**:
To stop seeding a payload that owes nothing more. Its files stay where they are. As a verb, "release" is only ever said of a payload.
_Avoid_: Remove, delete, finish

**Abandon**:
To stop following a grab that has not finished. What came of it stays with Downloads.
_Avoid_: Cancel, delete

### Who is asked

**Indexer**:
Whatever searches the trackers for releases of a book. It holds the tracker accounts; Prosody only asks it questions.
_Avoid_: Tracker, search provider

**Downloads**:
Whatever downloads a release and seeds it, knowing each by its grab.
_Avoid_: Client, torrent client

**Tracker**:
A site that lists releases and sets the obligation. Prosody never speaks to one itself.
_Avoid_: Indexer, source

**Torrent**:
Downloads' own word for a grab's download. Prosody knows one only by its grab.
_Avoid_: Using it for a release, a grab or a payload
