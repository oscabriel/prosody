# Prosody

One self-hosted app for ebooks and audiobooks: request a book, fetch it, shelve it, sync the audio to the text, and read or listen with one position per book.

Step 1 is done: a catalogue of the library, each book's text and audio aligned by the press and checked by ear, and a reading room where the audio follows the text and your place follows you between devices, served at prosody.example.com to your own devices on the tailnet. The app installs on the phone, plays from the lock screen, keeps books on the device to read and hear with no network, and opens on what you are reading now. A book with only its text reads, and one with only its audio plays, each with its cover. At the desk you want a book, grab one of the releases the indexer finds for it, and follow it through the download client to the shelf, where it pairs and aligns. A book kept only as a PDF opens a page at a time. A native iOS app comes next.
