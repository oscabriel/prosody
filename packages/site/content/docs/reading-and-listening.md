## The shelf

Prosody opens on **The shelf**, listing every book discovered in your library.

When you have books in progress, **On the nightstand** appears at the top of the shelf, ordered most recently opened first. Each entry shows `Last read on <device>, <date>`. Below it, **Every work** lists the full library alphabetically by author and title.

Each book on the shelf carries a status label:

- **Pairs (EPUB + M4B):** `synced`, `syncing`, `not synced` (or `not paired` before pairing starts). A `synced` book also notes what share of sentences were matched and how many spot checks passed.
- **Single editions:** `to read` (with `Text alone` or `Printed pages`), `to hear` (with `Audio alone`), `copying` or `not copied`.

## The reading room

Click any ready book on the shelf to open its reading room. The side panel shows the book's title, author, reading standing, offline status and **Contents**.

### Synced books

- **Listen / Pause:** Starts or pauses audio at your current place. As the narrator reads, the active sentence is underlined and marked in the margin (`☞`), and the page scrolls to keep it in view.
- **Tap any sentence:** Moves your place and seeks the audio to that sentence.
- **Read silently:** Scroll and read without audio; your place follows the sentence you read to. Pressing **Listen** later starts the narrator from there.
- **Speed:** The rate button beside the player steps through `1×`, `1.2×`, `1.5×`, `1.8×`, `2×` and `0.8×`. The player also shows how many minutes remain in the current chapter at the chosen speed.
- **Contents:** Lists every section of the book so you can jump between chapters.

### Text alone

An EPUB without an audiobook opens in the same reading room without the audio player. Your place is tracked by sentence.

### Audio alone

An audiobook without an EPUB shows its cover, chapter title, chapter clock and two skip buttons: **← Back 15 seconds** and **On 30 seconds →**, plus the standard **Listen / Pause** and speed controls. Your place is tracked by time.

### Printed pages

A PDF opens one page at a time, sized to fit the viewport:

- **Turn forward:** Tap the right two thirds of the page, swipe left, press `ArrowRight` or `PageDown`, or click **Page \<n\> →**.
- **Turn back:** Tap the left third of the page, swipe right, press `ArrowLeft` or `PageUp`, or click **← Page \<n\>**.
- **Zoom:** Pinch to zoom in. While zoomed in, dragging pans the page instead of turning it.
- **Contents:** Shows the PDF's top-level outline headings when present.

Your place is saved as a page number once you stay on a page for a second.

## One place across devices

Your place in each book lives on your host, not on a single device. Stop listening on your phone and the book opens at the same sentence on your laptop.

- Reading and listening share one place in a synced book.
- If you start a book as **Audio alone** and later pair it with its EPUB, opening the synced book lands on the sentence spoken at your saved timestamp.
- If two devices report different places, the newer timestamp wins.
- If another device moves ahead while a book is already open in front of you, a **Turn to where you were on \<device\> →** button appears at the top of the page.

## Finishing and setting aside

Opening any book puts it on the nightstand. While online, the reading room's side panel offers two buttons:

- **Set it aside:** Removes the book from the nightstand without losing your place. Opening it again puts it back.
- **Finished:** Marks the book finished and removes it from the nightstand while keeping your place.

Clicking either returns you to the shelf. Opening the book later shows `Finished on <date>` or `Set aside on <date>` in its side panel.
